Questions

Straight answers.

Will I know the price before you start?

Yes. We check the car, then call you with a clear price. Nothing gets done until you say yes.

Do I have to say yes?

No. If you'd rather think about it or get another quote, that's fine. No pressure.

What if you find something else wrong?

We stop and call you first. We explain what we found and what it costs, and you decide. It never just appears on the bill.

Can I see the old parts?

Of course. Ask and we'll keep them for you, or show you on the car.

Where are you based?

We work from home in Harlow, Essex. You bring the car to us, and we'll send you the address when you book. No big garage overheads, so you're not paying for a fancy reception.

Do you do MOTs?
