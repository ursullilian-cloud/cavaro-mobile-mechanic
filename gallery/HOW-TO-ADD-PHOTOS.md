# How to add photos to the website

Any photo you put in this `gallery` folder shows up in the "From the workshop" section of the website, usually within 1 to 2 minutes.

## Add photos (phone or computer)

1. Open https://github.com/ursullilian-cloud/harlow-motor-works/tree/main/gallery and sign in.
2. Tap **Add file**, then **Upload files**.
3. Drag your photos in (on a phone, tap "choose your files" and pick them from your gallery).
4. Tap the green **Commit changes** button.

That's it. Wait a minute or two, then refresh the website.

## Tips

- **Name the photo and it gets a caption.** A photo called `brake-job-ford-focus.jpg` shows the caption "Brake job ford focus". Photos with phone names like `IMG_4821.jpg` just show with no caption.
- **Newest on top:** photos are sorted by name, highest first. Phone names with dates in them (like `20261114_093012.jpg`) put the newest first automatically.
- **Use JPG, PNG or WEBP** photos. Smaller is faster: under 2 MB each is ideal. Most phones have a "medium" or "large" option when sharing, which works well.
- **Landscape or portrait both work.** The gallery fits any shape.
- **Don't show customers' number plates or faces** unless they've said it's fine.

## Remove a photo

1. Open the photo in this folder.
2. Tap the **...** menu (top right), then **Delete file**.
3. Tap **Commit changes**.

It disappears from the website within a couple of minutes.
